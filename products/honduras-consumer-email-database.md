## Market overview

With 580,000 verified contacts, the Honduras Consumer Email Database is one of LeadsBlue's consumer email list datasets for Honduras. Honduras has a GDP of approximately $34 billion (2023). Key industries include manufacturing and apparel (the maquiladora sector is the dominant formal-sector employer and generates approximately 35% of export revenue, primarily serving US apparel and textile brands), agriculture (coffee and bananas are the largest agricultural exports; Honduras is Central America leading palm oil producer), remittances (over $8 billion annually — approximately 25% of GDP), and mining. San Pedro Sula is the commercial and industrial capital, concentrating the majority of manufacturing, logistics, agribusiness, and private sector financial activity — it is the more active B2B hub than Tegucigalpa, the political capital. Honduras has one of the largest maquiladora sectors in the Western Hemisphere relative to its economy, making it a significant node in US apparel brand supply chains.

Honduran business culture is conducted in Spanish. English is spoken in maquiladora operations and by internationally educated executives in San Pedro Sula, but Spanish is necessary for effective B2B outreach to domestic companies. Business culture is relationship-driven, family-business oriented, and hierarchical. The informal economy accounts for approximately 50% of GDP, meaning formal B2B procurement is concentrated in the manufacturing, agribusiness, financial services, and telecoms sectors. Personal introductions are strongly preferred before formal outreach. Trust-building is a prerequisite for vendor relationship development — cold email performs best as an awareness tool followed by relationship warm-up rather than as a standalone conversion mechanism.

## Outreach strategy

Spanish is essential for all cold outreach to domestic companies. Subject lines should be formal and sector-specific, with manufacturing, agribusiness, and logistics being the most productive entry points. Best days for outreach are Tuesday through Thursday, 8am to 10am CST (Central Standard Time, UTC-6). Follow up 2-3 times over 3-4 weeks. Target San Pedro Sula for manufacturing, agribusiness, and logistics companies — Tegucigalpa for financial services and government-adjacent organisations. The maquiladora sector has the most structured procurement processes and is the most productive sector for international B2B vendors, particularly those offering operational technology, compliance solutions, or workforce services. A warm referral from a shared contact in the local network significantly improves conversion probability over unsolicited cold contact.

## How to use this list effectively

- **Verify before sending.** B2B contact data decays roughly 2–3% per month as people change roles. Run the list through a verification pass to remove stale or invalid addresses before your first send.
- **Segment by industry and role.** Response rates improve sharply when the message matches the recipient's vertical and seniority — use the segment fields to tailor your copy rather than sending one generic message.
- **Warm up the sending domain.** Start with low daily volume from a dedicated sending domain and ramp gradually to protect deliverability.
- **Follow a multi-touch cadence.** Most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch; a short sequence over 2–3 weeks outperforms a single send.
- **Respect compliance.** Include a valid postal address and a working opt-out, and honour unsubscribe requests promptly (see compliance notes above).
