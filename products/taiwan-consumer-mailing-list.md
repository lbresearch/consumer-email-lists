## Market overview

With 25,000,000 verified contacts, the Taiwan Consumer Mailing List is one of LeadsBlue's consumer email list datasets for Taiwan. Taiwan is a high-income economy with a GDP of approximately $800 billion and one of the world's most concentrated technology ecosystems. The island produces over 90% of the world's advanced semiconductors and hosts global leaders across chip manufacturing (TSMC), electronics (Foxconn, Asus, Acer), and precision machinery. Taipei's Neihu and Nangang districts are the primary tech and corporate hubs, while Hsinchu Science Park is the country's silicon valley equivalent. Taiwan's B2B market is characterised by dense SME networks, deeply integrated supply chains, and a manufacturing culture that rewards technical precision and long-term relationships over quick wins.

Despite its size, Taiwan's B2B purchasing community is relatively reachable — English proficiency is reasonable at senior levels, especially in technology and export-focused industries. Decision cycles in manufacturing and electronics tend to be longer but more predictable than in consumer markets. Taiwan operates in a complex geopolitical environment, which means international vendors who position stability and supply chain resilience as value propositions receive particularly receptive audiences.

**Key business hubs:** Taipei, Hsinchu, Taichung, Kaohsiung, Tainan.

## Outreach strategy

Cold email to Taiwan works best in English for technology and export-facing companies, and in Traditional Chinese for domestic-focused SMEs. Subject lines should emphasise technical capability, compliance certifications, or supply-chain value rather than cost savings. Timing: Tuesday to Thursday, 9am to 11am Taiwan Standard Time (UTC+8). Avoid the Lunar New Year period (January/February) and the Golden Week equivalent holidays. A first email introducing capability, a second with a specific case study from the semiconductor or electronics vertical, and a third with a demo invitation is a reliable 3-touch structure.

## Compliance

Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) governs commercial email. For B2B email to corporate accounts, legitimate business interest applies when the email is relevant to the recipient's professional role. Consent is required for marketing to individuals. The enforcing authority is the National Development Council. Taiwan's PDPA penalties include criminal liability for intentional violations — maximum NT$2 million (approximately $63,000 USD) per breach.

## How to use this list effectively

- **Verify before sending.** B2B contact data decays roughly 2–3% per month as people change roles. Run the list through a verification pass to remove stale or invalid addresses before your first send.
- **Segment by industry and role.** Response rates improve sharply when the message matches the recipient's vertical and seniority — use the segment fields to tailor your copy rather than sending one generic message.
- **Warm up the sending domain.** Start with low daily volume from a dedicated sending domain and ramp gradually to protect deliverability.
- **Follow a multi-touch cadence.** Most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch; a short sequence over 2–3 weeks outperforms a single send.
- **Respect compliance.** Include a valid postal address and a working opt-out, and honour unsubscribe requests promptly (see compliance notes above).
