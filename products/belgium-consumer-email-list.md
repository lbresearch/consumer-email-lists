## Market overview

With 4,300,000 verified contacts, the Belgium Consumer Email List is one of LeadsBlue's consumer email list datasets for Belgium. Belgium is a small country with outsized strategic importance — Brussels hosts the headquarters of the European Union, NATO, and over 1,400 international organisations and NGOs, making it one of the most important political and institutional B2B markets in the world. Antwerp is Europe's 2nd largest port and the global centre of the diamond trade; Ghent and Liège are significant industrial cities. The Belgian business community is trilingual (Dutch-speaking Flanders, French-speaking Wallonia, German-speaking East Cantons) and highly internationally oriented given its geographically central position in the EU.

**Key business hubs:** Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, Liège, Bruges, Leuven.

## Outreach strategy

Belgian B2B cold email requires language awareness — Dutch for Flemish companies, French for Wallonian companies, and English for Brussels-based EU institutions and multinationals. English is widely understood in business contexts across all regions. Belgian executives are pragmatic and process-oriented; emails that are clear, structured, and reference compliance or regulatory context (particularly relevant given the EU institutional context) perform well. The EU institutional sector in Brussels has highly specific procurement processes — direct cold email to EU officials is less effective than participating in formal tender processes.

## Compliance

Belgium follows GDPR, enforced by APD (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens / Autorité de Protection des Données — the Belgian Data Protection Authority, bilingual by law). The APD follows EDPB guidance allowing legitimate interest for B2B cold email to corporate addresses where professionally relevant. Belgium's Electronic Communications Act regulates commercial electronic communications. The APD is a moderately active enforcement authority.

## How to use this list effectively

- **Verify before sending.** B2B contact data decays roughly 2–3% per month as people change roles. Run the list through a verification pass to remove stale or invalid addresses before your first send.
- **Segment by industry and role.** Response rates improve sharply when the message matches the recipient's vertical and seniority — use the segment fields to tailor your copy rather than sending one generic message.
- **Warm up the sending domain.** Start with low daily volume from a dedicated sending domain and ramp gradually to protect deliverability.
- **Follow a multi-touch cadence.** Most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch; a short sequence over 2–3 weeks outperforms a single send.
- **Respect compliance.** Include a valid postal address and a working opt-out, and honour unsubscribe requests promptly (see compliance notes above).
